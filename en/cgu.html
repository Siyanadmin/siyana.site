Using Siyana means accepting these Terms and the Siyana charter, available in the app (Help & support) and on this website. If you disagree, please do not use the app.

  • Listener: a user who listens to and reads content.
  • Publisher: an imam or speaker (lecturer, teacher, association) whose account has been approved and who can publish.
  • Content: any audio or text published on Siyana (sermon, lecture, class, reminder), with its information (title, description, topic, language, references).

4. Imam and speaker accounts

  • Before his first publication, every imam goes through a compulsory training mode.
  • An imam can ask to switch to a speaker account. A speaker account can never become an imam account again.

  • respect French law and the Siyana charter;
  • use the “sisters only” label only for content truly suited to it. Only women speakers can use it; it is mandatory for a Quran recitation, tajweed or memorisation published by a woman speaker.

The following are strictly prohibited:

Each publisher is solely responsible for the content they publish.

  • Content reserved for sisters is only offered to women users.

7. Reporting, moderation and sanctions

  • Any content can be reported from the app, with a reason. A person can only report the same content once. Reports must be made in good faith.

The request takes effect 28 days later; until then, simply signing back in cancels it. Reminder messages are sent during this period.

  • In the event of the death of an imam or speaker, their content remains published. Their family may, on presenting proof, ask for it to be removed and the account closed, or kept with a discreet mention on their profile.
