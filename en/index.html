A library of the spoken word

Siyana brings together sermons, lectures, classes and reminders published by imams and speakers. A place for listening, designed for passing on knowledge and preserving the spoken word, in serenity.

For listeners

  • Listen for free, at any time, even offline.
  • Search by topic, imam or mosque.
  • Follow your imams and mosques to be notified of their publications.

For imams and speakers

  • Record your words directly from the app: every recording is protected, even if the connection drops or a call comes in.
  • Every account is checked and approved by our team, together with a guarantor mosque.
  • You remain the owner of your content and decide how it is shared.

Our commitment

  • No advertising, no sale of data.
  • No likes, no comments, no algorithm: content is shown from newest to oldest.
  • Every report is reviewed by a human.
